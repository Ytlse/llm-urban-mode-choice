Toulouse: cleansing staff begin an open-ended strike today

This Monday, waste has been removed nowhere in the commune of Toulouse. The staff responsible for cleansing are on strike, an open-ended strike as of today. Since this morning they have been blockading the authority's two depots: Raisin, in the centre, and Monlong, in the Saint-Simon district. Nothing has left either site since 5 a.m. As a result, not one container has been emptied today. "A strike that could last," warn the union representatives at Monlong.

The reason for the anger? The staff are protesting against the new removal timetable, announced by management and the metropolitan authority, applicable as of today, 18 March 2019. What changes? Household waste will be removed twice a week instead of three times — except in the inner centre, where there is no space to put containers on public land. This change of frequency alters the removal days, and so the working rhythm of the staff, who are now also required to clock in on public holidays. That is what is going down badly.
