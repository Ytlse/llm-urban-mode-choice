Squalls above 80 km/h: Toulouse closes its parks and gardens at short notice this Thursday evening

With Haute-Garonne placed under a yellow storm warning, the city council has decided to close its parks and gardens from 6 p.m. this Thursday 16 July. "A weather alert alerts us to violent winds," the council says on its website.

Every green space in the municipality is affected. Among them are the Abadie, Maurice-Bécanne, Capucins, Compans-Caffarelli and Embarthe gardens, along with the Fontaine-Lestang grounds. The public will likewise be unable to enter the Grand Rond, the Job park, the Michelet garden, the Niel garden, the Claude-Nougaro garden or the botanical garden.

Other emblematic sites — the Poudrerie, Raymond-VI and Royal gardens, the grounds of the Reynerie château, the Saint-Sernin square — also remain shut for the duration of the alert.

The ban will hold for as long as weather conditions put the public at risk. According to the council, the gates of these spaces will reopen to residents only once the warning is lifted. Municipal staff must first inspect each site to make sure there is no danger, in particular from falling branches or trees.
