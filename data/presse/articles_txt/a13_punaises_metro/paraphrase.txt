Several videos posted on TikTok in recent days have raised concerns about bed bugs on the Toulouse metro. The footage is blurry, but the people who published it say they filmed the parasite there.

The clips have left residents uneasy, wondering whether the insect has taken up residence in the carriages. Dark shapes can be made out on the walls and on the fabric seating, though it is impossible to say with certainty what they are.

Faced with these worries, the network operator was asked to clarify matters. It says no official complaint or report has been recorded. As a precaution, it has nonetheless decided to act, to guarantee the safety and well-being of the public.

The operator stated: "As a precautionary principle, we have introduced canine detection. If the dogs pick up any sign of them, we step in at once with a 180-degree steam treatment to remove every trace of these parasites."
