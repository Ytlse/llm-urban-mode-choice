The programme for La Machine's new show: the Giants will clash once more in this second part of the urban opera

The giant machines are coming back to the heart of Toulouse. From Friday 25 to Sunday 27 October, the urban opera "The Guardian of the Temple" will give its second part, "The Gate of Darkness". Here is the weekend programme, and the list of places to stand to make the most of the event.

The spider, the Minotaur and a newcomer, the guardian of darkness, will clash in the heart of Toulouse. An extraordinary urban opera by the La Machine company. In 2018, the first part of "The Guardian of the Temple" drew 900,000 visitors.

In this second part, Asterion the Minotaur, guardian of the pink city, and Ariane the spider face a newcomer: Lilith, guardian of darkness. This open-air show will unfold in the heart of Toulouse over three days. Three days for a play in three acts.

As the day goes on, prophetic and prodigious signs appear in the city. On the banks of the river, three prodigious signs herald the imminent opening of the gate of darkness.
