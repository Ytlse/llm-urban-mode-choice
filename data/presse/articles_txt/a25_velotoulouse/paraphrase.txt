Soft mobility in Toulouse: the new VélôToulouse service launches on 30 August

The Mayor of Toulouse and the president of the organising authority took advantage of the opening of the two structures crossing the arm of the île du Ramier, this Saturday 22 June, to unveil the look of the new vélôToulouse.

The service, open to the public from 30 August 2024, will offer a renewed and more varied fleet. The current offer, with 283 stations and 2,600 bicycles, will grow to 400 stations and 3,300 bicycles, half of them with electric assistance, in orange, and half without assistance, in pink. "The share of assisted bicycles may change over the life of the contract, up to 75% of the fleet," the president explains.

Work on the 117 new stations began in late March 2024: it involves preparing the anchorings and fitting the new furniture. The neighbourhoods concerned will be told before work starts, to keep disruption to a minimum. "From May to August 2024, the 283 existing stations will be modernised without any interruption of service," the deputy mayor responsible assures us.
